About this tutor

This is an independent study companion for the InterSystems ObjectScript Specialist exam. It turns the Study Pack into a guided set of chapters you can work through at your own pace.

  • Study the explanations and examples, then check your understanding with practice questions.
  • Apply the ideas in worked cases and track your progress as you go.
  • Use the final assessment to review your answers by exam area.

Your progress is saved in this browser. The course index includes options to export it to a backup file or import it in another browser.

This is an independent learning resource and is not an official InterSystems website or certification resource. Check the official exam outline for current exam information.
